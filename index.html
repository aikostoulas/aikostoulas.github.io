Research toolReaLiTea

Reflexive Interview Companion

A companion for carrying out a research interview, from preparation to secure storage of the data. It combines practical checklists for before, during and after the interview with reflexive prompts at each stage, so you can notice how you shape the conversation and what to do differently next time.
